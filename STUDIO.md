# Aether Studio — locked visual system

Wordmark: **AETHER**
Legal: Aether Studio
Line: One-of-one digital flagships. Designed with Grok. Owned by you. Deployed from GitHub.

Rename candidates (if the founder rejects Aether): Meridian Atelier, Palladium, Northline, Provenance.

## Thesis
A commissioned company site should feel like an object — ink, metal, paper — not a template wearing a logo.

## Audience + emotion
Founder-led companies that sell expensive things. Quiet certainty. “These people take this as seriously as we do.”

## Type
- Display: Instrument Serif
- Text: Instrument Sans
- Mono: Geist Mono (repo / deploy moments only)

## Color
- Ink `#0C0B09` — dominant field
- Paper `#EDE6D9`
- Brass `#C4A574` — jewelry, never fills
- Smoke `#8A8276`
- Field `#141210`

## Material
Rag paper, brass type, darkroom grain. Hairline rules. No glassmorphism, no orbs, no purple-blue AI gradients.

## Motion
Slow editorial rise. 900ms cubic. Almost still. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Grid
Asymmetric editorial. Numbered rails. Wide margins. Lists over card grids.

## Photography direction
Empty rooms, dusk exteriors, metal and paper stills. No stock smiles. No handshakes.

### Imagine prompts

- Composition: 16:9, object left-third, large negative field
- Light: single north window, late afternoon
- Lens / film: 85mm, Portra 400 grade
- Color: ink, paper, brass only
- Negative: no neon, no screens, no people, no logo mockups in chrome browsers
- Aspect: 16:9

**Work plate — Vale & Stone**
- Subject: Concrete and oak interior of an unfinished pavilion, one chair
- Composition: centered vanishing point, high ceiling

- Negative: no furniture catalog look, no HDR
